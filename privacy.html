Privacy Policy

Karamyshev Blog Publisher is a personal tool used only by its owner, Alex Karamyshev, to upload and schedule videos on his own YouTube channel.

Data we access

With the owner's consent the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and publish dates on the owner's channel. It reads basic channel information only to confirm the upload goes to the right channel.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is collected from other users, sold, or shared with third parties.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
